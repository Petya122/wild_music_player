Wild Music Player plays the music that's already on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and no internet connection at all: everything stays on your device.

🎵 YOUR LIBRARY
• All your songs, albums, artists and folders, with sorting and fast search
• Favorites, Most Played, Recently Added and your own playlists
• Drag to reorder, rename, undo, and play any list in order or shuffled
• Listening stats: your top songs, artists and albums
• Edit song details: fix the title, artist, album or cover inside the file

🎤 LYRICS
• Synced lyrics that follow the song, from your own .lrc files
• Pick a lyrics folder once, or load a file for a single song
• Fix the timing if a line comes early or late

🎚️ SOUND
• Equalizer with presets, plus a decibel boost for quiet recordings
• Volume leveling so quiet and loud songs sound alike
• Smooth crossfade between songs
• Sleep timer

🚗 CAR & BLUETOOTH
• Song titles on your car's display
• Press Play on the car radio or headset to continue where you left off
• Music continues when your car reconnects
• Separate sound settings for each Bluetooth device: car, headphones, phone
• Android Auto support

📱 EVERYWHERE ON YOUR PHONE
• Home screen widgets: the player, and a one-tap playlist button
• Quick Settings tile to start or pause from anywhere
• Long-press the app icon for Shuffle all, Favorites or your last playlist
• Lock screen and notification controls

🎨 MADE FOR YOU
• Light, dark and true black themes with accent colors
• Big-font friendly
• Back up your playlists and favorites to a file, and restore them on a new phone

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Wild Music Player has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere. It only reads your music files to play them.
